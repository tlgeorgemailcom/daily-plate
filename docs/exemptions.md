# Examples of Exemptions

Ingredients in the following categories may be marked as exempt in ingredient-by-ingredient linking. The moderator will review all exemptions.

---

## Spices and seasonings

Spices and seasonings without nutritional value are exempt. Salt is excluded from this category — sodium may become a queryable nutrient in a future release and should be linked where possible.

- Black pepper, white pepper, cayenne, paprika, chilli flakes
- Cumin, coriander, turmeric, fenugreek
- Cinnamon, nutmeg, cloves, allspice, cardamom, star anise
- Dried herbs: thyme, oregano, rosemary, sage, bay leaf, tarragon, marjoram, dill
- Spice blends used in trace amounts (e.g. a teaspoon of mixed spice, a pinch of five-spice)
- Vanilla extract used as a flavouring (by the teaspoon)

---

## Leavening agents

- Baking powder
- Baking soda (bicarbonate of soda)
- Cream of tartar
- Yeast (dry or fresh, used for rising — not as a nutritional supplement)

---

## Functional cooking agents

Ingredients used in the cooking process that are not meaningfully consumed in the finished dish:

- Cooking spray
- Parchment paper or baking paper (not consumed)
- Water or ice used as a processing step only (e.g. blanching water, ice bath) — not water that is served as part of the dish
- A poaching, blanching, or braising liquid that is discarded before serving
- Food coloring (gel, liquid, or powder with no nutritional contribution)

---

## Not exempt

The following could be misidentified as exempt but should be linked:

- **Salt** — excluded from the spices exemption; sodium is a tracked nutrient
- **Butter, oil, or fat** used in cooking — these contribute calories, fat, and fat-soluble nutrients
- **Sugar, honey, maple syrup** — significant caloric and carbohydrate contribution
- **Flour, starch, or breadcrumbs** — significant macronutrient contribution
- **Dairy (milk, cream, cheese)** — linked to its as-eaten form
- **Any protein (meat, fish, eggs, legumes, tofu)** — always linked
